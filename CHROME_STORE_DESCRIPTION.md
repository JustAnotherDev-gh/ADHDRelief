# Chrome Web Store Listing for ADHD Relief

## Short Description

Focus & relax with ambient sounds: brown noise, nature sounds, lofi beats. Continuous mode or timed break mode.

## Detailed Description

ADHD Relief helps you focus, relax, and be more productive with ambient sounds designed for concentration.

Perfect for people with ADHD, students, remote workers, developers, and anyone who needs help focusing or unwinding.

🎵 MULTIPLE SOUND OPTIONS

Choose from 7+ built-in sounds:
• Brown Noise - Deep, soothing low-frequency sound
• Pink Noise - Balanced across all frequencies
• Grey Noise - Psychoacoustically balanced
• Rain - Gentle rainfall ambiance
• Ocean Surf - Calming ocean waves
• Forest Waterfall - Peaceful nature sounds
• Lofi Loop - 6 curated lofi hip-hop beats

Plus: Upload your own custom sounds (My Vibe) - perfect for your favorite music, nature recordings, or white noise variations.

🧠 SMART FOCUS MODES

Continuous Mode: Sound plays continuously for deep work sessions
Break Mode: Sound plays ONLY during breaks - silence during focus time. Perfect for the Pomodoro Technique!

⏱️ BUILT-IN TIMER

• Pomodoro preset (25 min focus / 5 min break)
• Extended focus (50 min / 10 min)
• Custom durations
• Gradual 5-second fade-in during breaks
• Visual phase indicator

🔒 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE

• NO account required
• NO tracking or analytics
• NO internet connection needed after installation
• All sounds generated locally in your browser
• Your custom audio files stay on your device
• What you hear stays on your machine

WHO IS THIS FOR?

✓ People with focus challenges
✓ Students studying for exams
✓ Remote workers and developers
✓ Anyone who works in noisy environments
✓ Meditation and relaxation
✓ Better sleep preparation
